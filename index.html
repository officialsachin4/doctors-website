Meet Dr. Farooq Khan

A Consultant Hepatologist & Gastroenterologist with advanced UK training

Dr. Farooq Khan is a highly distinguished, UK-trained Consultant Hepatologist & Gastroenterologist in Dubai, bringing extensive expertise and an innovative approach to digestive and liver health.

His career has focused on advanced patient care, medical education, hepatology and interventional endoscopy.

He completed the comprehensive Trent GI programme in the UK, followed by advanced Liver Transplant training at St. James Hospital in Leeds. This experience provides a strong foundation for managing complex liver conditions and delivering comprehensive patient-centred care.

Endoscopic Ultrasound (EUS)
